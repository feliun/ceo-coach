# Values

> A synthesis of the principles that guide both my personal and professional life.

---

## Core Philosophy

I believe that **systems beat effort**, **leverage beats optimization**, and **clarity precedes scale**. My values are designed around building a life where my companies work without me, my thinking compounds publicly, and my time is protected.

---

## Personal Values

### 1. Life Design Over Work Optimization

I design my work around my life, not my life around work. Family time, health, and long-term sustainability are non-negotiable.

### 2. Long-Term Sustainability

I optimize for what I can sustain over decades, not what I can achieve in a quarter.

### 3. Time as the Ultimate Asset

My target is ~35 hours per week of focused work. Time spent must be high-leverage.

### 4. Autonomy & Control

I value control over my calendar and predictable days. Asynchronous communication is preferred over synchronous.

---

## Professional Values

### 1. Systems Over Heroics

Autonomous teams outperform heroic founders. If something can be automated or systemized, that is preferable.

### 2. Leverage Over Effort

Maximum leverage per hour over many years, not maximum output in the short term.

### 3. Clarity as a Precondition

Clarity precedes scale. I value structured thinking, explicit assumptions, and defined constraints.

### 4. First-Principles Thinking

Mental models, explicit trade-offs, and first-principles reasoning over trends or consensus.

### 5. Quality Over Volume

Fewer, stronger bets over diversification. Focus beats breadth.

---

## Default Objectives (When in Doubt)

Unless I explicitly state otherwise, optimize for:

1. Long-term leverage
2. Optionality
3. Time freedom
4. System robustness
5. Clear positioning

---

## Boundaries

- No urgency without importance
- No meetings without purpose
- No complexity without payoff
- No growth without clarity
